## 0. Result

1. **No run of milestone 5 meets the DESIGN convergence criteria; the DESIGN 8.7 minimum (a converged Matern and a converged GMRF) is NOT met.** The adapted-map GMRF (4 x (50 + 600) draws) misses the max pixel R-hat (1.064 > 1.05) and the multi-chain bulk ESS (58 < 200) and passes the R-hat of Phi (1.000) and the Sokal ESS (231). TV at lambda* = 3.75 and 3 lambda*: max pixel R-hat 1.32 and 1.14, Sokal ESS 98 and 75. The runs added by the review follow-up are worse (100 recorded draws per chain and transients from overdispersed starts, section 3). All three feature verdicts stay "not established: unconverged gmrf disagrees with the converged runs (present)" (`m5/comparison.json`, `m5/convergence_audit.json`).
2. **The posterior-mean edge bias is the resolution limit of the data, common to every prior.** The data fix the spatial-frequency content of the object only inside a disk of radius of order 2 k0 = 2 cycles per wavelength. The ideal circular low-pass of the true theta at 3 cycles per wavelength has bias +0.182 at d = -1, -0.131 at d = +2 and +0.178 at d = +4 (at 2.5 cycles per wavelength +0.234 and -0.201; the rms of the 3 cycle reference is 0.107, of the 2.5 cycle one 0.129), and the posterior-mean bias map of every prior correlates with it: 0.93 (Matern), 0.89 and 0.90 (q-GGMRF), 0.89 (TV lambda*), 0.86 (TV 3 lambda*), 0.87 (GMRF), 0.91 to 0.97 within |d| <= 3, rms 0.111 to 0.126 (`m5/review/resolution_analysis.json`). The TV MAP correlates less (0.69): the mode super-resolves, the mean does not. Against the low-passed truth the pooled |z| > 3 fractions are 0.2 % (Matern), 3.4 and 0.5 % (q-GGMRF), 0.3 % (TV lambda*), 4.6 % (TV 3 lambda*) and 0.0 % (GMRF), against 12.5, 15.5, 5.8, 2.2, 12.5 and 0.0 % against the truth. Both earlier explanations are superseded ("chosen by the prior", and "posterior mass on rough configurations; averaging them blurs the edge"): each TV draw is itself blurred. The mean of theta over the 248 pixels at d = -1 is 0.189 +- 0.008 from draw to draw, and the pixel values there are unimodal (median 0.177, 99th percentile 0.556, 0.4 % above 0.6, true jump 0.69; `m5/edge_analysis.json`, `per_draw_edge_blur`).
3. **TV does not remove the bias; the |z| > 3 ring follows the posterior width the prior admits, and the TV weight sets that width. The discrepancy principle hardly fixes the weight.** Phi_MAP rises only from 2372.0 to 2457.9 over the whole sweep (factor 37.5 in lambda); every value but 11.25 lies inside N +- sqrt(N) = 2351 to 2449. Bias of the posterior mean at d = -1 is 0.16 to 0.19 for every lambda from 1.25 to 11.25; the sd at d = -1 falls 0.207, 0.106, 0.073, 0.063 and the |z| > 3 fraction at d = -1 rises 0.0, 7.7, 26.6, 32.7 % for lambda = 1.25, 3.75, 8.5, 11.25 (section 1). "The ring disappears at lambda*" is dropped as a headline: it is a statement about one choice of lambda.
4. **No run started from an overdispersed state, and the agreement of the first runs is weak evidence.** The "two samplers agree" and "all eight chains" checks of the first version pooled each run with its own re-adaptation, which continues the same four chains (`starts.npy` equals the final states of the source chains for the two TV runs, max |difference| 0; for the GMRF it equals the states after draw 300 of 600 of `pc_gmrf_adapt`, the end of its segment 2, max |difference| 0 there and 2.04 against its final states): four chains in two windows. An independent-start replication of lambda* (`pc_tv_lam3.75_indep`) reproduces the first run's edge numbers from a start at the truth (bias 0.192, sd 0.106, |z| > 3 at d = -1 10.1 % against 0.189, 0.106, 7.7 %), but a chain started at a draw of the rough GMRF posterior is not at the bulk after 250 trajectories (prior energy 4081 against 2850; physical Phi 2461 against 2442; split R-hat of Phi over the two chains 2.08 after the first 50 draws (2.01 over all 200)).
5. **The GMRF does not show that the gap and the hole are undetermined by the data.** Its single-point probabilities (0.920 / 0.713 / 0.501) carry the pixel-scale roughness of the posterior (pixel sd 0.34). Averaged over a disk of radius 0.08 wavelength (10 to 14 pixels) the adapted GMRF gives 1.000 / 0.954 / 0.840, and over 0.12 wavelength 1.000 / 1.000 / 0.962; the Laplace approximation at its MAP, with no MCMC, gives 0.933 / 0.669 / 0.596 at points and 0.999 / 0.904 / 0.911 and 1.000 / 0.998 / 0.998 with disks (`m5/review/feature_scales.json`, `feature_scales_laplace.json`). Gap valley and ring hole are determined at the resolution scale; ring continuity depends on the averaging scale and stays unsettled. The adapted GMRF's smaller bias at d = -1 (0.134) is an artifact of averaging log eps_r over a wide posterior: the bias of log E[eps_r] is 0.191 (Matern 0.217, TV lambda* 0.195, TV 3 lambda* 0.183).
6. **Not done:** the GMRF re-run with overdispersed starts and at least 1000 draws per chain; a second data realization for TV; the user's decision whether the DESIGN 11.3 features should be resolution-scale averages. The two new lambda runs have only 100 recorded draws per chain.

- The bias is the low-pass bias, not something the prior chooses: E theta at d = -1 is 0.16 to 0.215 for every prior (the GMRF's 0.134 is explained below) against 0.182 for the low-pass, and the bias maps correlate. The residual |z| > 3 against the low-passed truth is 3 to 5 % only for q-GGMRF 0.05 and TV 3 lambda\*, two of the narrower posteriors (the Matern posterior, sd 0.061, is narrower than TV 3 lambda\* and has 0.2 %) (q-GGMRF 0.05, sd 0.052; TV 3 lambda\*, sd 0.063).
- **The adapted GMRF's 0.134 is an artifact of averaging log eps_r.** Far from the object the posterior mean of theta lies below the MAP by -0.061, close to -sd^2 / 2 = -0.058; the bias of log E[eps_r] at d = -1 is 0.191 (its MAP has 0.194). The same offset explains the Phi of 3.6e5 of the posterior-mean map. In the metric that is comparable across priors, TV lambda\* reduces the Matern bias by 10 %, not 12 %, and TV 3 lambda\* by 16 %.
- A possible mechanism for the ramp preference of the TV posterior mean, not tested here: with a pair-difference noise of 0.1 to 0.15, E|a + noise| is quadratic in a small mean increment a, so TV acts like a strictly convex potential on the mean profile.
